Reconstruction of the legacy CS TA/Reader application form

Rebuilt by reading the PHP source, not by running it. Labels, headings and drop-down options are copied from the code as written. Blue notes explain repeated rows and anything filled in at runtime. Nothing on this page submits.

Where this comes from (apps/taapp)
  • index.php builds a TAform and calls show_form(); the browser title is "My First TA App" for a new application, "TA Application" when editing.
  • includes/ta_form_class.php holds nearly everything:
    • Option lists (courses, grades, residency, language exam, majors, class level, years, skills, job preferences, duties) and row counts: lines 15–460
    • show_form(), section order and the comments box: line 1286
    • show_instructions(): 1624 · show_personal_info(): 1689 · show_courses(): 1598
    • show_coursework_other(): 1069 · show_coursework_290(): 1112 · show_knowledge(): 1660
    • show_experience(): 1226 · show_desired(): 1157 · show_preferences(): 1757
    • Required-field checks in validate_form(): 1795 (first/last name, email, both GPAs)
  • includes/html_functions.php: page_head() line 24 and page_tail() line 142 (header image, logout link, footer).
  • includes/sql_functions.php: get_ta_dates() line 703 reads the availability dates from the config table.
  • taapp.css for the look. Older copies ta_form_class.php-20240723 and -20260729 differ only in the course list.

CS TA/Reader Application Form

Instructions:

By submitting an application for employment for this position, you as the applicant authorize the CS Department as the hiring department to access your academic record to confirm your current UCSB enrollment status and related academic good standing University eligibility for student employment.

Please fill out all sections. You may return to the application as many times as you want to edit your application before the application deadline stated in each quarter's application call. After the application deadline, applications will be reviewed and students should therefore not edit their applications further.

  • All entries in the "Personal Information" section are required
  • Please do not submit an application if you are not serious about considering an offer to be a TA/Reader
  • Please keep your comments to a minimum. Comments are cut off after 250 characters maximum including spaces.
  • If you're currently taking a course, please choose 'IP' (In Progress) as the grade.

Important notices:

Each quarter's TA/Reader application announcement is sent by the CS Student Affairs Manager via email to the CS graduate student list-serv approximately 6-8 weeks before the start of a quarter.

Completion of this application form does not, in itself, guarantee that you will be offered a TA/Reader position. Applications will be reviewed and considered within the scope of all of the following factors: instructional budget resources, past experience, past student/supervisor evaluation results, and academic good standing eligibility within the University.

TA/Readers are required to be available from [available_from_month day] to [available_to_month day]. If you are not available to be employed as a TA/Reader for the entire period of these dates for the quarter, do not apply for a TA/Reader position.

The dates come from the database config table. If they were not set, this whole instructions block was skipped.
